Where Australian Postcards Cluster

Discover geographic concentrations in the SLNSW collection.
Each hexagon covers approximately the same ground area.
Darker blue indicates more catalogue records in that cell.

Source: State Library of New South Wales, Miscellaneous Australian Postcards. The map aggregates 1,195 mapped catalogue records into 145 occupied equal-area hexagons. Only high-confidence title-based locations are included.

Hexagons were constructed in an Australian equal-area coordinate system at approximately 50 km width. Coastal cells may extend beyond the shoreline. Counts do not represent postcard production or population.

How SLNSW Postcards Are Catalogued

Explore how SLNSW records are organised by scope and format.
Each rectangle represents a catalogue format.
Larger rectangles contain more catalogue records.

Select a record scope to examine its formats.
Hover over rectangles for exact counts and percentages.

Source: State Library of New South Wales, Miscellaneous Australian Postcards. The treemap covers 1,559 catalogue records. Counts are grouped using catalogue scope and format labels.

A grouped or folder record may describe multiple postcards. Rectangle area therefore represents catalogue records, not the number of physical postcards. Tooltip percentages refer to all 1,559 records.

Data sources: National Museum of Australia, State Library of New South Wales, and Austrian National Library AKON.

Map boundaries: Australian states GeoJSON/TopoJSON; Natural Earth world boundaries.
