Postcard Places Across New South Wales

Circles show mapped SLNSW catalogue records by locality.
Larger circles represent more catalogue records.
Locations are matched localities, not exact postcard sites.

Source: State Library of New South Wales, Miscellaneous Australian Postcards. Localities use high-confidence title-based matches. Counts refer to catalogue records, not physical postcards.
